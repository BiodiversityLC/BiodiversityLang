COIL CRAB

SiguMasons edibility rating: Hard asrocks and taste like dirt F

Scientific name: Carabus caput
Coil-crabs as they're now referred as, are part of a rapidly mutating population of Uca rapax discovered on the asteroid moons 21-Offense and /736)///ch//. //heir specific mutation seems to have come about due to being exposed to the remains of destroyed Vir colligerus on the two moons. 

Their outter shells have become softer due to the intense radiation, and as a result have begun to wear Vir colligerus as a hermit crab would their home; due to how volatile and dangerous these are, Carabus caput lacks any natural predators. More over, their claws have become so large and deformed that they've become useless for hunting large prey.

They hunt by to immitating the behavior of active Vir colligerus, pursuing their prey at highs speeds but moving only when out of sight; they however don't sem to care if individuals other than their target stare at them during pursuit.
