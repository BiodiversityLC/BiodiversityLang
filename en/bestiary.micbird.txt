Scientific name: Buccina-manta
Relatives of the manticoil, they inhabit both hot and cold environments and start their day during the early hours of the morning. Buccina-manta are gluttonous creatures who ravage large portions of land in search of food. They migrate very often due to their natural predators catching wind of their presence and or due to a lack of food left in the area. Boom birds have been found to have a strange affinity for strong LEDs, and enjoy being around electronics.

A lack of pack mentality and direction has caused them to develop a huge foghorn-like structure around their mouth, which allows them to call out to others of their kind for mating and regrouping. After a large quantity of them have settled into a new area, they will rest until the morning and destroy the local flora and mega-fauna.
